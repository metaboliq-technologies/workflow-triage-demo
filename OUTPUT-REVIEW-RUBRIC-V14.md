## Representation/validation limits

v12 is unchanged. v14 factorizes all original word/punctuation token endpoints instead of enumerating a sampled window list; no64-option truncation. It can represent same-field token-aligned spans within120, not arbitrary intra-token slices. Model selects one sufficient fact span, or exactly two nonoverlapping facts for multiple_intents; deterministic smallest enclosing span becomes the public quote. Reason/category agreement, count, token existence/order/field, overlap, length and a narrow English trailing-determiner cut check are enforced; self-claimed unavailable earns no credit. These are structural checks, not a semantic oracle: two spans may still describe the wrong or same intent, omit a critical qualifier, choose the wrong reason, or be misleading. An English endpoint heuristic may reject legitimate wording; cross-field/discontiguous-only evidence cannot be stitched. Larger token-ID enums increase prompt/schema size; long-input/provider schema limits remain unmeasured by these<=500-unit bodies. Reason/fact claims are model assertions, not independent verification. All such failures and uncertain reviewer judgments must be reported.

One small targeted English synthetic run cannot establish significance, stable failure rates, causal gain, representative production readiness, human validation, training exclusion or mathematical shortest-span minima. Keep historical comparisons separate.
